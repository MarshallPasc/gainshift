# What's new in Gainshift

## 2.0.2

- **New name.** Tab Volume is now Gainshift. Your saved sites carry
  over — nothing to set up again.
- **Muting looks like muting.** Turn a tab down to 0 and the toolbar
  button shows a mute icon — a speaker with an X — instead of a red
  "0". Hovering over it says "muted".
- **Fixed:** turning the volume down didn't always stick on sites that
  set their own volume. Browser games and music players were the usual
  culprits. It holds now.
- **Fixed:** if you saved a site at 0%, reopening the panel showed
  100% even though the tab was silent — and touching the slider then
  unmuted it.
- **Fixed:** a level you set on one site could follow you to the next
  site you opened in that tab.
- **Fixed:** boosting above 100% is more reliable, and when it can't
  work the panel now gives the real reason instead of a guess. Usually
  it's that the site handles its own audio and won't let anything else
  near it.
- The number in the panel no longer flickers back to an old value
  while you drag the slider.
